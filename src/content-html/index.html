ABOUT CLIFF KINCHEN
He doesn’t teach

leadership theory.

He teaches what he lived.

“Most leaders confuse authority with leadership. They have people who comply — not people who commit.”

Cliff Kinchen built his foundation over two decades of leadership in the United States Air Force — not as a title, but as a proving ground. Serving as a Law Enforcement Supervisor, Defensive Tactics and Firearms Instructor, and Air Base Ground Defense Instructor, he didn’t simply fill these roles — he elevated them, shaping the standards others were measured against.

That same commitment carried into his role as Superintendent and Acting Director of Education at one of the Air Force’s largest Non-Commissioned Officer Academies, where he developed over 1,200 military and civilian leaders every year. His impact earned him recognition as Senior NCO of the Year for Air Education and Training Command — selected from a field of nearly 8,000 eligible Senior NCOs across the largest command in the U.S. Air Force. He was also his command’s nominee for the 12 Outstanding Airmen of the Year — the Air Force’s most prestigious enlisted recognition program.

Running parallel to his military career, Cliff pursued martial arts not just as a physical discipline, but as a study of mindset, resilience, and leadership rooted in decades of tradition. That path led him to earn an 8th-Degree Black Belt, claim the Joe Lewis Fighting Systems U.S. Light Heavyweight Title, and win the International Fighters Association North American Super Middleweight Title — distinctions that reflect not only technical mastery but the mental discipline and strategic precision that define his approach to leadership.

Today, Cliff brings both worlds together through The Kinchen Standard — a seven-pillar leadership framework he developed from the ground up, drawn from thirty years of military service, martial arts, and frontline leadership under real pressure. He works with organizations, teams, and individuals who are ready to stop managing people and start leading them.

Senior Platform Engineer  |  Executive Coaching Client

Executive coaching client — career clarity, decision-making, and leadership under pressure

“When I walked into Cliff’s dojo I was carrying a lot I had not really looked at. Pressure at work, financial stress, and grief I had not processed yet. I started training one month before I lost someone close to me, and I did not realize until later that something had pointed me to that dojo right before the floor dropped out.

What changed under Cliff’s instruction is not the weight. Life still piles on. What changed is how I stand under it. He teaches you to hold your center. When something hits, you do not lean back and scramble. You stay rooted, take what you can, redirect what you cannot, and come back to center. I use that off the mat far more than I use it on it. I do not scramble anymore. I plant my feet and deal with what is actually in front of me.

A lot of those decisions went through conversations with Cliff after class. He never told me what to do, and that is the part most people would not expect from someone with his background. He has every reason to hand out orders. He doesn’t. He asks the right questions, makes sure I am looking at the full picture, and lets me come to my own conclusion. He helped me get clear on career moves I had been sitting on for too long, and on financial decisions I had been avoiding because they felt too big to look at directly. I make decisions cleaner now, and I follow through on them. That is on him.”
